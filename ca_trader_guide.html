📖 Complete Step-by-Step CA Trader User Tutorial

Master all capabilities of CA Trader, from institutional multi-timeframe charting and algorithmic option contract selection to live paper order execution, trailing stops, and Sentinel post-trade analysis.

1. Workspace Navigation & Section Bar Navigation

The top navigation section bar contains all operational modules. On desktop, click any tab. On mobile, horizontally swipe across the bar to browse modules, then tap the module you wish to enter (sliding will not open unwanted tabs). The active panel will smoothly highlight. Use the ⚡ Turbo Refresh button at any time to execute an instant, zero-reload refresh across all connected WebSockets and REST feeds.

2. Professional Canvas Charting & Touch Controls Interactive Chart

The central chart is powered by a high-frequency Canvas renderer. Switch timeframes between 1m, 5m, 15m, 1h, and 1D. Use the + and - zoom buttons or pinch on mobile (pinch-in zooms in; pinch-out zooms out). Tap the Crosshair icon to reveal price and timestamp axes with clean single badges. To draw trendlines or support levels, choose a drawing tool. If you want to move drawings, unlock the 🔒 Lock button—unlocked drawings feature a 24px touch tolerance for easy mobile repositioning. All applied indicators and custom drawings automatically save to your browser's localStorage.

3. Multi-Factor Recommendations, Mathematical Weightages & Strict Consensus Algorithmic Signals

CA Trader does not rely on a single indicator. It cross-examines 4 quantitative pillars using an institutional weighted scoring matrix:

1. Technicals (35% Weightage)
20 EMA Trend Alignment (10%) · 50/200 EMA Regime (8%) · Supertrend (7%) · RSI-14 (5%) · MACD Momentum (5%)
2. Greeks & Order Flow (30% Weightage)
Delta Alignment (10%) · Gamma Acceleration (8%) · Theta Burn Tolerance (7%) · PCR & OI Build-up (5%)
3. News Catalysts & Materiality (20% Weightage)
High-Materiality (75–100%) Financial News (12%) · Gemini AI Qualitative Digest (8%)
4. Global Macro Drivers (15% Weightage)
GIFT Nifty Overnight Index Premium (6%) · Crude Oil (Brent/WTI) (4%) · DXY & US 10Y Yields (5%)
Explicit Exclusions (What the Engine Rejects):
• Social media rumors, unverified Telegram tip alerts, and anonymous pump-and-dump chatter.
• Deep OTM lottery options with wide bid-ask slippage (>0.5%) and zero market maker presence.
• Stale news older than 48 hours or unverified blogs lacking institutional citations.
• Holiday overnight spread distortions and low-volume illiquid price wicks.

Directional Consensus Rule: When the underlying index or future is Bullish, the engine strictly recommends Call (CE) near-ATM options. When Bearish, it strictly recommends Put (PE) options. In the recommendation display, the primary consensus contract is actively highlighted while the opposing direction is displayed as theoretical/inactive.

📚 Exhaustive Trading Terms & Options Greeks Dictionary Searchable · 25+ Institutional Concepts
Delta (Δ) Option Greek
Measures the expected change in option price for every ₹1 change in the underlying spot price. Calls range from 0 to +1.0; Puts range from -1.0 to 0. ATM options typically have a Delta of ~0.50.
Formula: Δ = ∂V / ∂S · If Nifty rises 100 pts, a 0.50 Delta CE gains ~₹50.
Theta (Θ) & Hourly Burn Rate Option Greek
Measures the rate of time decay of an option's extrinsic premium over 24 hours. Accelerates drastically in the final 72 hours before expiry. Option buyers lose Theta daily, while option sellers gain it.
CA Trader Sentinel: Hourly Burn = Daily Theta / 6.25 trading hours.
Gamma (Γ) Option Greek
Measures the rate of change of Delta for every ₹1 move in the underlying asset. Highest for At-the-Money (ATM) options and causes explosive premium surges during expiry day breakouts.
Formula: Γ = ∂²V / ∂S² · High Gamma turns a 0.30 Delta option into 0.80 quickly.
Vega (ν) Option Greek
Measures the sensitivity of the option premium to a 1% change in Implied Volatility (IV). When IV surges (before earnings or RBI policy), both CE and PE premiums expand.
Formula: ν = ∂V / ∂σ · High Vega options suffer when volatility crushes post-event.
Implied Volatility (IV) Pricing Variable
The market's forecast of the underlying asset's likely movement over the coming year, annualized. Derived backwards from the market price using the Black-Scholes formula.
High IV indicates expensive options; Low IV indicates cheap premium acquisition.
Peak P&L (High-Water Mark) Sentinel Metric
The absolute highest unrealized mark-to-market profit achieved by an open trade before any reversal occurred. Tracked live on every tick in the positions.peak_pnl database column.
Example: If a position reaches +₹500 and retraces to +₹200, Peak P&L remains recorded at +₹500.
Put-Call Ratio (PCR) Order Flow
The ratio of total Put open interest divided by total Call open interest across all strikes. PCR > 1.2 suggests an oversold market ready for a bullish bounce; PCR < 0.7 suggests overbought conditions.
Formula: PCR = Σ Put OI / Σ Call OI.
VWAP (Volume Weighted Avg Price) Technical Benchmark
The average price an asset has traded at throughout the day, weighted by volume. Institutional buyers use VWAP as their primary execution benchmark. Prices above VWAP indicate bullish intraday control.
Formula: VWAP = Σ(Price × Volume) / Σ(Volume).
RSI (14-Period Momentum) Oscillator
A momentum oscillator that measures the speed and magnitude of recent price changes on a scale of 0 to 100. RSI > 70 indicates overbought momentum; RSI < 30 indicates oversold exhaustion.
Bullish Momentum Confirmation: RSI holding above 50 during pullbacks.
EMA Confluence (20 / 50 / 200) Trend System
A type of moving average that places greater weight and significance on the most recent data points. CA Trader examines the 20 EMA for short-term trend, 50 EMA for intermediate support, and 200 EMA for structural regime.
Golden Alignment: Price > 20 EMA > 50 EMA > 200 EMA signifies strong institutional trend.
Execution Slippage Market Microstructure
The difference between the expected price of a trade and the actual price at which the order is executed on the exchange. Typically caused by fast volatility or thin bid-ask spreads.
Mitigation: Use limit orders or near-ATM liquid strikes with high trading volume.
Supertrend Indicator (10, 3) Trailing Volatility
A volatility-based trailing stop indicator calculated using the Average True Range (ATR) multiplied by a factor of 3. Changes color from green to red when price penetrates the dynamic stop level.
Formula: Upper Band = (High + Low)/2 + (3 × ATR); Lower Band = (High + Low)/2 - (3 × ATR).

🏛️ System Architecture Analysis & Recommendations
1. Is our backend & frontend too long?

Yes, significantly.
• app.py: 12,386 lines (~701 KB)
• terminal.html: 18,111 lines (~1.1 MB)

Currently, both files are 100% monolithic. All features (auth, database, chart rendering, math calculations, news scraper, Sentinel advisor, admin passbook) reside in single mega-files.

2. How to save AI tokens and time during edits?

When files are 18,000 lines long, sending or reading them consumes hundreds of thousands of tokens per prompt.

Actionable Strategies:
• Modularization: Split app.py into routers/ and services/. Editing a 150-line options router costs 95% fewer tokens than opening app.py.
• Targeted Instructions: Specify the exact function name (e.g. options_summary) or DOM ID so the AI agent reads only 50 lines.

3. Can we use Django to improve the database?

Django ORM & Migrations: Django brings robust schema versioning (`makemigrations`), foreign keys, and an out-of-the-box admin panel.

Best Architecture Approach: Rather than discarding FastAPI's ultra-fast asynchronous WebSocket stack, implement SQLAlchemy ORM + Alembic migrations inside FastAPI. This gives you identical relational power, automated migrations, and connection pooling without rewriting the WebSocket engine.

4. Live Logging & Visual Error Monitoring

Replace raw console prints with structured logging:
• Backend: Use loguru with auto-rotating file handlers (`ca_trader.log`, max 50MB, retain 7 days).
• Frontend Visual Error Tracker: Add a global window.onerror hook that automatically reports unhandled JS exceptions and layout overflow warnings back to the backend `/api/logs/client-error` so they appear live in the Admin Passbook!
