CA Trader Interactive Terminal Guide
Expand any module `[+]` / `[-]` to explore operational workflows, formulas, hotkeys, and algorithmic mechanics.
1 1. Real-time Candlestick Charts & Advanced Overlays
[-]
Professional high-frequency charting engine powered by Upstox Market Stream v2.
  • Multi-timeframe: 1m, 3m, 5m, 15m, 30m, 60m, 1D with instant sub-millisecond switching.
  • Indicators: EMA (20/50/200), SMA, Bollinger Bands, Supertrend, RSI (14), ATR, VWAP, MACD.
  • Interaction: Freeform Pan mode (drag chart horizontally & vertically), Crosshair mode with dynamic price/time tags, reset view, and latest candle fast-forward.
  • Fullscreen: Dedicated fullscreen experience with floating indicator and drawing parameter tooltips.
2 2. Candlestick, Trend & Chart Pattern Recognition Scanner
[+]
Automated institutional pattern recognition scanner operating across all timeframes simultaneously.
  • Rule-Based Logic: Strict multi-candle verification (e.g. Doji after uptrend requiring a breakdown close below Doji low to confirm SELL signal).
  • Expanded Signatures: Morning Star, Evening Star, Engulfing, Three White Soldiers, Marubozu, Head & Shoulders, Double Top/Bottom, Triangles.
  • Auto Navigation: Clicking any detected pattern scrolls directly to the chart and highlights the formation candles with a 10-second auto-reset timer.
  • Scan Range: Defaults to previous trading session open (09:15) through current session close (15:30) with 60-second auto-refresh.
3 3. CA AI Unified Recommendations & Risk Geometry
[+]
Unified master consensus engine combining deep neural NLP news sentiment with technical price action.
  • Trade Geometry: Precise mathematical calculation of Entry, Dynamic ATR-based Stop Loss, Target 1, Target 2, and Risk-to-Reward Ratio.
  • Formula Proof: Tap any price level to inspect the algorithmic formula, indicator parameters, and pivot references.
  • Session Management: Distinct Active Intraday / Next-Day Setups versus historical audited trades.
  • Square-Off: Intraday positions display explicit square-off timestamps (15:15 IST) and exit timestamps.
4 4. Real-time Option Chain, Greek Surface & F&O Finder
[+]
Comprehensive options matrix for NSE equities, indices, and MCX commodities.
  • Black-Scholes Greeks: Real-time computation of Delta, Gamma, Theta, Vega, and Implied Volatility (IV).
  • ATM Highlight: Dynamic At-The-Money strike tagging synchronized with live underlying spot ticks.
  • Buyability Finder: Ranks option strikes by risk-adjusted momentum, liquidity, and theta burn safety.
  • MCX Support: Native commodity option chain support for Crude Oil, Natural Gas, Gold, and Silver contracts.
5 5. CA AI News Intelligence & Interactive Counter-Questioning
[+]
Institutional news filter that screens noise and extracts market-moving financial catalysts.
  • External Links: Click any news headline to read the original article directly from verified financial publishers in a new tab.
  • Interactive AI Discussion: Click any news card to open an interactive chat modal where you can query CA AI on option implications, volatility spikes, and trade setups.
  • Sentiment & Materiality: 0–100% impact probability scoring distinguishing macro global events from single-stock disclosures.
6 6. Point-in-Time Backtesting & Historical News Replay
[+]
High-fidelity tick replay engine that lets you re-live past trading sessions without lookahead bias.
  • Zero Lookahead: Indicators, patterns, and signals are computed candle-by-candle strictly using past minutes.
  • News Replay: Live stream of bullish and bearish news events published before the replay timestamp.
  • Controls: Play, pause, single-step advance, speed multipliers (1x, 2x, 5x, 10x), and interactive scrubbing timeline.
7 7. Segregated Funds Passbook & Balance Management
[+]
Three independent, segregated wallets protecting your capital.
  • Segregated Wallets: Trading Wallet, Testing Wallet, and Auto-Trade Wallet.
  • Live Passbook: Detailed audit ledger displaying transaction timestamp, reference ID, debit/credit badges, amount, and post-transaction balance.
  • Instant Reset: Reset balances back to fresh ₹1,00,000 baseline with one click for clean backtesting.
8 8. Performance Reports, Tax P&L & Audit Trail
[+]
Complete institutional reporting suite.
  • P&L Metrics: Net Realized P&L, Win Rate %, Turnover, Profit Factor, and Maximum Drawdown.
  • Tax Estimation: Automatic computation of STT, GST, and exchange transaction fees.
