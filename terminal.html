CA Trader Tutorial
A complete quick-start guide to every major workflow.
1. Watchlists & Search Create multiple watchlists, reorder instruments, search symbols/contracts and use suggestions. Selecting a stock updates the terminal context.
2. Charts & Technicals Change timeframe, use indicators, patterns, crosshair and drawings. Tap a timeframe signal to inspect the technical basis.
3. Dashboard Review price, Change %, technical consensus, material news, global impact, option context, risk and trade-ready highlights in one layout.
4. Options & Greeks Inspect the chain, select strikes, view Greeks, use Buyability Finder and monitor watched option Greeks through notifications.
5. Recommendations Compare technical, CA Trader and CA AI views. Use on-demand CA AI when you want a deeper decision-support opinion.
6. News Intelligence Global and stock news exclude generic market updates. Select multiple articles to analyze, classify Positive/Negative/Neutral, delete, or analyze all.
7. News Feed Swipe through individual material stories, understand why each appeared, open its source, add it to the main News panel, or analyze it with CA AI.
8. External News Paste a headline, summary, URL, or any combination. CA Trader returns up to five candidates; select exactly which story to add.
9. Market Movers View live gainers, losers, volume and turnover rankings. Rankings are recalculated from current quotes when the tab is opened/refreshed.
10. Auto Trade Configure capital, maximum loss and eligible stocks. Tap a suggestion to see its evidence before adding it.
11. Orders & Risk Review paper orders, positions, stops, targets and risk checks. Live execution remains controlled.
12. Bodybuilder Terminal Track meals, quantities, macros, micronutrients, editable diet entries, workout logs and exercise-level PRs.
13. Notifications The bell surfaces important events, including monitored option-Greek changes, technical changes and qualifying news.
